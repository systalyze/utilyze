Utilyze is rolling out as a DaemonSet. It runs on nodes labeled nvidia.com/gpu.present.

Check collector health:

  kubectl logs -l app.kubernetes.io/name={{ include "utilyze.name" . }} --tail=20

View live metrics from one node on your workstation:

  kubectl port-forward pod/<pod-on-that-node> 8079:8079
  utlz --connect ws://127.0.0.1:8079/live
